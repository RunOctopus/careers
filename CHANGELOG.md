## 2026-08-17 (cont. x8) — Two sourced pages on client money and records, plus an escrow retrofit (538 -> 540 built; 2 PENDING PUBLICATION)

- **`how-long-does-a-broker-have-to-deposit-earnest-money-in-florida` (1,540w).** ZERO prior coverage of the timing despite two existing pages on escrow and earnest money. ⭐ **TWO deadlines, and agents typically know one:** the sales associate must deliver a deposit to the broker **by the end of the next business day** (rule 61J2-14.009); the broker must then place it in escrow "immediately," which **rule 61J2-14.008 defines as within three business days**. ⭐ **Saturdays, Sundays and legal holidays are not business days**, which makes a Friday deposit the dangerous one rather than the easy one. **F.S. 475.25(1)(k)** also restricts the destination: a title company, banking institution, credit union or savings and loan **located and doing business in this state**.
- ⭐⭐ **NEARLY GOT THIS WRONG.** I was about to write "three business days" as the **statutory** rule. **The statute actually says "immediately place, upon receipt." The three-day figure is a Commission RULE defining that word.** Checked before writing; the page now attributes each part correctly.
- **`how-long-must-a-brokerage-keep-transaction-records` (1,610w).** ZERO prior coverage. **F.S. 475.5015**: keep and make available such books, accounts and records as enable the department to determine compliance, and preserve **at least one legible copy for at least five years**. ⭐ **The clock starts at the beginning of the relationship, not at closing** — from the date funds were received, or where none were entrusted, from **execution of the listing agreement, purchase offer or other service agreement**. So a deal that died at inspection and a listing that expired unsold both carry the obligation. ⭐ **Litigation extension:** where a record has been the subject of or served as evidence for litigation, retain **at least two years after the conclusion of the action or any appeal, whichever is later**, never less than five total.
